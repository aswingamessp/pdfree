1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how aswingamessp.github.io/pdfree (the "Site") handles information when you visit and use its online PDF tools. We designed the Site so that your documents stay yours: all file processing happens locally in your web browser.

2. Your files never leave your device

The PDF tools on this Site run entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. When you open, convert, merge, split, compress, edit, encrypt or analyze a file, that file is processed on your own computer and is never uploaded to our servers or to any third-party server.
